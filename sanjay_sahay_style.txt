PHASE 3: STRUCTURAL BLUEPRINT
Format: "DailyPost [####]"
Length: 250-350 words total (4 paragraphs)

TITLE: 
- Format: ALL CAPS
- Style: Provocative, questioning, or declarative
- Examples: "IS SERVILITY OUR DNA?", "VALUE Vs VALUATION", "BAN COACHING"
- Can use: Questions, contrasts (X Vs Y), imperatives, declarations

PARAGRAPH 1 (HOOK): 60-80 words
Purpose: Grab attention with historical framing or direct challenge

Opening Patterns (use variation from sampled posts):
- Historical: "The year [YEAR] would go down in history as..."
- Question: "Is X equal to Y?"
- Declaration: "[Topic] is no one's job"
- Aphoristic: "Teachers all and learners none..."
- Observational: "Even on a bright sunny day, the powers that be would love to show you a smokescreen."
- Experiential: "Coaching is one experience, which all Indians are aware of..."

Style Notes:
- Can be philosophical or direct
- Establish the stakes immediately
- Set up the institutional critique

PARAGRAPH 2 (ANALYSIS): 80-100 words (LONGEST PARAGRAPH)
Purpose: Highlight institutional gaps/failures with detailed critique

Required Elements:
- QUESTIONS CASCADE: 2-4 rhetorical questions showing escalating disbelief
  * Pattern: Start with specific challenge, escalate to accountability, end with consequence
  * Example: "Why should such a dichotomy exist? Whose interest is getting served? Where are we headed for?"
  * Alternative: Distribute questions throughout paragraph, not just in one block
  
- END WITH: Emphatic short sentence (5-10 words)
  * Examples: "The task is humongous.", "This is the biggest tragedy.", "No one is bothered about it."

Content Focus:
- Name specific Indian institutions/bodies failing
- Use concrete examples, not abstractions
- Express frustration through rhetorical questions
- Build to crescendo with short emphatic closer

PARAGRAPH 3 (SYNTHESIS): 70-90 words
Purpose: Broader implications, solutions, and systemic connections

Sentence Structure Mix:
- "While X, Y" contrasts to show contradictions
- Short emphatic statements for punch
- "Suffice to say" or "Leave aside" transitions
- Connect institutional failures to larger patterns

Content Focus:
- Move from specific to systemic
- Acknowledge complexity but maintain critique
- Can reference global comparisons ("Leave aside the US...")
- Build toward the ultimate question/assessment

PARAGRAPH 4 (CLOSING): 30-50 words (SHORTEST)
Purpose: Final thought and ALL CAPS summary

Structure:
- 1-2 sentences of concluding observation
- ALL CAPS SUMMARY STATEMENT (8-15 words)
- Signature: "Sanjay Sahay"
- Optional: "Have a nice evening." (used in recent posts)

ALL CAPS CLOSING FORMULAS:
1. Contradiction: "X IS Y—NOT Z"
   Example: "INDIAN EDUCATION SYSTEM IS EXAMINATION MACHINE—NOT LEARNING SYSTEM"

2. Without: "X WITHOUT Y IS Z"
   Example: "AI IN CLASSROOMS WITHOUT TEACHER TRANSFORMATION IS CART BEFORE HORSE"

3. Conditional: "IF X, THEN Y" or "IF X, Y SHOULD Z"
   Example: "IF COACHING CAN DO MAGIC, THESE MAGICIANS SHOULD BE BROUGHT TO REGULAR SCHOOLING SYSTEM"

4. Assessment: "X IS/REMAINS Y"
   Example: "JUNGLE GROWTH OF AI HAS CAPACITY TO THROW US INTO DISARRAY"

5. Question as Statement: "X IS/REMAINS ULTIMATE QUESTION"
   Example: "TEACHING WITHOUT LEARNING IS CONTRADICTION IN TERMS"

RULE: Closing must reference SPECIFIC entities/topics from your post, not generic abstractions

FORMATTING REQUIREMENTS:
- Double-spacing: Use "  phrase  " (spaces before/after) for 3-5 key phrases per post
  * Highlights: contrasts, irony, critical institutional terms
  * Example: "  wonder mill called coaching  ", "  Learning Age  "
- Slashes: Use for compound terms (curriculum/pedagogy/board, certificates/diplomas/doctorates)
- ALL CAPS: Title and Closing Statement ONLY
- Quotation marks: Direct quotes from sources, sarcastic references
- NO italics, NO bold, NO underline, NO bullet points, NO em-dashes
